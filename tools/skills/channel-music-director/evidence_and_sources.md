## Verification procedure
1. Open the platform's own sound page/editor before publishing. Check credited artist and exact remix/slowed version.
2. Check trend freshness in the last 7–14 days in the intended region/platform; label verified rising only if source supports it.
3. Check rights for account type: personal vs business and ad/monetized use.
4. If a song is unavailable, use a properly licensed mood-equivalent rather than uploading an unauthorized recording.
5. Avoid quoting unverified metrics or calling an audio 'viral today' based on a 2025 clip.

## Note about royalty-free
'Royalty-free' rarely means 'copyright-free.' Verify license terms, attribution, modifications, commercial use, and platform-specific restrictions for each recording.
