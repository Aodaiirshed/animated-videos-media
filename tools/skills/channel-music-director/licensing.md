# Copyright and sourcing guardrails

This archive deliberately contains NO MP3/WAV copies of popular songs. Most named songs are copyrighted and cannot be freely redistributed.

For publication:
- YouTube Shorts: use music included and permitted in the Shorts creation tool or another recording with explicit rights for the intended use. Library selection does not necessarily license the same recording for a separate platform or a long-form upload.
- Instagram Reels: add platform-authorized audio natively when applicable; account type, commercial use, ads and geography may change availability. Do not assume Instagram's music library grants TikTok/YouTube reuse rights.
- TikTok: for commercial/promotional content, review its Commercial Music Library or procure suitable rights; native personal-account catalog use is not universal permission.
- Cross-posted exports: use a recording you can prove is licensed on ALL intended platforms (or export clean narration and add music separately within each platform).
- Creative Commons / other free recordings: read the specific license. Some require credit, prohibit commercial use or restrict modifications; keep proof/source URL.
- Never use downloader/ripping workflows to evade copyright restrictions.

Sources for lawful alternatives to investigate: YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay Music (inspect track-level terms), Mixkit (inspect per-track license), and platform-provided commercial libraries. If the user owns or licenses recordings, Claude may work with lawfully supplied files.
